# Documentation editing

Follow the public writing rules in [docs/README.md](README.md#文档写作与更新规范).

- Lead with the conclusion and scope; keep the benchmark main score separate from diagnostic and planning measures.
- Write for readers, not as a chronological experiment log. Put methods in the appropriate guide and avoid repeating them in the report.
- State table units, direction, denominator, coverage, and source; distinguish measured findings from interpretation and open limits.
- Describe unavailable artifacts and what the evidence does not establish. Auxiliary coordinate readouts do not establish physical accuracy.
- Treat generated BEGIN / END marker blocks as script-owned. Never hand-edit generated values; use the generator and its check mode.
- Preserve frozen scores and version identities. Verify links and relevant render checks after editing.
